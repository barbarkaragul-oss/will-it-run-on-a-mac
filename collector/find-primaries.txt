# find primaries and operators: primary<TAB>expression words (tab-separated)
# Each expression is executed on every platform as `find . <words...>` in a scratch directory that holds
# probe.txt, stdin from /dev/null, and classified by the platform's own unknown-primary wording, learned
# from a canary (`find . -wiroamnosuch`). Arguments are chosen to be valid on every platform that has the
# primary, so a rejection is about the primary, not its argument.
# Union of GNU findutils, BSD (macOS) find and BusyBox find. Not here: -context (SELinux) and -d (a BSD
# option before the path, a deprecated GNU alias after it; the exhaustive short-flag probe covers it).
